4. One important change from my previous suggestion

I would not manually create every sliding window in this preprocessing script.

Chronos-2's current pipeline.fit() takes the time-series inputs and internally constructs its training dataset using Chronos2Dataset, including context_length, prediction_length, and min_past.

So your data-preparation layer should remain:

Yahoo Finance
     ↓
clean daily prices
     ↓
log returns
     ↓
long-format series
     ↓
train.parquet
validation.parquet

Then:

train.parquet
     ↓
Chronos2Pipeline.fit()
     ↓
sliding training contexts

That keeps the pipeline much cleaner.
